    Source: ICBC Vehicle Population open data (public.tableau.com/profile/icbc), full row-level extracts for passenger & commercial vehicles, 2021–2025. Counts are vehicles with an active policy as of Dec 31 each year, by the owner's mailing-address community.
    Regional districts: communities are rolled up to BC's 27 regional districts (MVRD, CRD, FVRD, CVRD, etc.) by municipality and named locality; about 98.5% of vehicles map to a district. The small remainder (unresolved “Unknown” postal codes and the RD-less Stikine Region) is grouped as “Unassigned / other localities.” Search the box by name or abbreviation.
    Size & length: each of ~11,000 models is assigned to a size class (from make, body style and model name) with a representative length; “fleet length” sums those. Net weight is a measured field in the ICBC data. Length reflects the fleet’s mix; weight also captures growth within a class. Read model-year trends knowing older vehicles still on the road skew toward trucks/vans (survivorship).
